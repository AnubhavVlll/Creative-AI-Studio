## 🤖 Product Overview

CreativeAI Studio brings multiple AI-powered content workflows into one workspace, supporting the journey from:

**Idea → Story → Images → Video → Narration/Music → Final Content**

The product is designed to reduce fragmented workflows and provide greater control over AI-powered content production.

## 🎯 Key Product Challenge

The core challenge was translating a high-level goal—**“create a complete children’s story”**—into a connected product experience involving multiple AI capabilities, dependencies, review stages, and safety requirements.

## 🧠 Key Product Decisions

- Open-weight/open-source-first strategy for core AI capabilities
- GPU-based AI infrastructure for greater model and infrastructure control
- Centralized handling of asynchronous AI generation jobs
- Review checkpoints before generated content moves to the next stage
- Phased MVP approach to control product complexity
- Child-safety requirements for the expanded child-facing experience
